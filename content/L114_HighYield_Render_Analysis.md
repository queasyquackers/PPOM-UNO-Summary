```text
• Hyperechoic- increased echogenicity- brighter 
• Isoechoic- similar echogenicty to background tissue
•  Hypoechoic- decreased echongenicity- darker 
• Anechoic- black (simple fluid) 
• Shadowing (reflective)- the beam is reflected back (calcium/bone)
```
