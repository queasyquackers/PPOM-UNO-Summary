# Lecture 70: Pediatric H&P I - High Yield Render

## 1. Developmental Milestones (The "Must Memorize" Table)
*Rules of 3s often apply.*

| Age | Gross Motor | Fine Motor | Language | Social |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2 mo** | Head up (45 deg) | Tracks midline | Coo | Smile |
| **4 mo** | Roll front->back | Grasp rattle | Laugh | Regard hand |
| **6 mo** | **Sit** (Tripod) | Transfer object | Babble | Stranger anxiety |
| **9 mo** | **Crawl**, Pull to stand | **Pincer** (Cheerio) | "Mama" (nonspecific) | Object Permanence |
| **12 mo** | **Walk** | 2-finger pincer | "Mama" (specific) | Drink from cup |
| **15 mo** | Walk backward | Stack 2 blocks | 4-6 words | Spoon/Fork |
| **18 mo** | Run, Stairs | Stack 4 blocks | 10-25 words | Remove clothes |
| **2 yr** | Jump | Stack 6 blocks | **2-word phrases** | Parallel play |
| **3 yr** | Tricycle | **Circle**, 9 blocks | 3-word phrases | Group play |
| **4 yr** | Hop | **Cross** | Telling stories | Dress specific |

## 2. Primitive Reflexes
*Persistence = CNS Pathology (e.g., CP).*

*   **Moro:** Disappears **4 mo**.
*   **Rooting:** Disappears **4 mo**.
*   **Palmar Grasp:** Disappears **3 mo**.
*   **ATNR (Fencing):** Disappears **6 mo**.
*   **Babinski:** Normal up to **12-24 mo**.

## 3. Newborn Pathology Buzzwords
*   **White Pupillary Reflex** $\rightarrow$ **Retinoblastoma** or Cataracts.
*   **Sunken Fontanelle** $\rightarrow$ **Dehydration**.
*   **Bulging Fontanelle** $\rightarrow$ **ICP** (Meningitis).
*   **Crosses Suture Lines** $\rightarrow$ **Caput Succedaneum** or Subgaleal (bad).
*   **Does NOT Cross Sutures** $\rightarrow$ **Cephalohematoma**.
*   **Single Umbilical Artery** $\rightarrow$ **Renal/Chromosomal complications**.
*   **Delayed Cord Separation** (>14d) $\rightarrow$ **Leukocyte Adhesion Deficiency**.
*   **Bilious Vomiting** $\rightarrow$ **Malrotation w/ Volvulus**.
*   **Failure to Pass Meconium** ($>$48h) $\rightarrow$ **Hirschsprung's** or **CF**.

## 4. Screening Timeline
*   **Metabolic:** Birth.
*   **Anemia/Lead:** 12 months.
*   **Autism (M-CHAT):** 18 & 24 months.
*   **Vision/Hearing:** 4 years.
*   **Lipids:** 9-11 years.

## 5. Hip Dysplasia
*   **Risk:** Female, Breech, Fam Hx.
*   **Tests:** Barlow (Bad/Dislocate), Ortolani (Good/Reduce).
*   **Dx:** US (<4mo), X-ray (>4mo).
*   **Tx:** Pavlik Harness.
