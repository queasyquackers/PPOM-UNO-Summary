## 1. General Principles
*   **Common Theme:** Accumulation of protein aggregates (inclusions) resistant to degradation.
*   **Toxicity:** Aggregates elicit stress responses, damaging neurons.

## 2. Information on Specific Diseases

### 2.1 Alzheimer's Disease (AD)
*   **Epidemiology:** Most common cause of dementia in elderly.
*   **Clinical:** Slow onset memory loss $\rightarrow$ disorientation $\rightarrow$ severe variability/immobility.
*   **Pathology:**
    *   **Gross:** Global Atrophy (Widened sulci, Narrowed gyri). **Hydrocephalus ex vacuo** (ventricles dilate to fill space).
    *   **Microscopic:**
        1.  **Senile (Neuritic) Plaques:** Extracellular. Core of **Beta-Amyloid (A$\beta$)**.
            *   Derived from **Amyloid Precursor Protein (APP)** cleavage by $\beta$-secretase and $\gamma$-secretase.
            *   **A$\beta$42** is the toxic, aggregation-prone form.
        2.  **Neurofibrillary Tangles (NFTs):** Intracellular. Hyperphosphorylated **Tau protein** (microtubule-associated).
            *   **Correlation:** Number of **Tangles** correlates better with degree of dementia than plaques.
*   **Genetics:**
    *   **APP (Chr 21):** Down Syndrome patients get AD by age 40 (Trisomy 21 = extra APP gene).
    *   **Presenilin 1 (Chr 14) & 2 (Chr 1):** Early-onset familial AD (increase A$\beta$42).
    *   **ApoE4 (Chr 19):** Increased risk for sporadic late-onset AD.

### 2.2 Frontotemporal Lobar Degeneration (Pick Disease)
*   **Clinical:** **Signification behavioral/personality changes** (disinhibition) precede memory loss. Aphasia.
*   **Gross:** **"Knife-Edge" Atrophy** of Frontal and Temporal lobes. Spares Parietal/Occipital.
*   **Microscopic:** **Tau inclusions** (Pick Bodies) in some variants (FTLD-Tau). Spheroidal inclusions.
*   **Note:** FTLD-TDP43 is another variant (Ubiquitin/TDP-43 inclusions), associated with ALS.

### 2.3 Parkinson's Disease (PD)
*   **Pathophysiology:** Loss of **Dopaminergic neurons** in the **Substantia Nigra** (Pars Compacta).
*   **Clinical (TRAP):**
    *   **T**remor (Resting, "Pill-rolling").
    *   **R**igidity (Cogwheel).
    *   **A**kinesia / Bradykinesia (Slow movement, shuffling gait, masked facies).
    *   **P**ostural Instability.
*   **Microscopic:** **Lewy Bodies** (Intracellular eosinophilic inclusions with halo).
    *   Composed of **$\alpha$-Synuclein**.
*   **MPTP:** Contaminant in illicit opioids $\rightarrow$ Acute Parkinsonism (destroys Substantia Nigra).

### 2.4 Huntington's Disease (HD)
*   **Genetics:** Autosomal Dominant. **CAG Trinucleotide Repeat** expansion in *HTT* gene (Chr 4).
    *   **Anticipation:** Repeats strictly increase during spermatogenesis (Paternal transmission $\rightarrow$ earlier onset in next gen).
*   **Pathology:** Atrophy of the **Caudate Nucleus** (and Putamen). Loss of GABAergic neurons.
*   **Clinical:** **Chorea** (jerky, purposeless movements), Dementia, Depression/Suicide. Presents in 30s-40s.

### 2.5 Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS)
*   **Definition:** degeneration of **Upper Motor Neurons (UMN)** in cortex and **Lower Motor Neurons (LMN)** in spinal cord/brainstem.
*   **Clinical:**
    *   **LMN signs:** Weakness, Atrophy, Fasciculations.
    *   **UMN signs:** Hyperreflexia, Spasticity, Babinski sign.
    *   **Sensation is intact.**
*   **Genetics:** Familial cases often linked to **SOD1 mutation** (Superoxide Dismutase 1).

### 2.6 Prion Disease (Creutzfeldt-Jakob Disease - CJD)
*   **Mechanism:** Conversion of normal Prion protein (**PrP$^{c}$**, $\alpha$-helix) to pathogenic form (**PrP$^{sc}$**, $\beta$-sheet).
    *   PrP$^{sc}$ induces conformational change in normal PrP$^{c}$ (chain reaction).
*   **Pathology:** **Spongiform Encephalopathy** (vacuoles in gray matter). No inflammation.
*   **Clinical:** **Rapidly Progressive Dementia**, Startle Myoclonus. Death < 1 year.
*   **Types:** Sporadic (most common), Variant (Mad Cow/BSE), Familial, Iatrogenic.

## 3. Peripheral Neuropathies

### 3.1 Charcot-Marie-Tooth (CMT) Disease
*   **Etiology:** Hereditary Motor and Sensory Neuropathy (HMSN). Auto Dominant (PMP22 duplication is common).
*   **Clinical:** Distal muscle atrophy (**"Stork leg" deformity** / Inverted/Champagne bottle legs), **Pes Cavus** (High arch), Hammer toes.
*   **Microscopic:** Repetitive Demyelination/Remyelination $\rightarrow$ **"Onion Bulb"** formation. Palpable nerves.

### 3.2 Diabetic Neuropathy
*   **Patterns:**
    1.  **Distal Symmetric Sensorimotor:** "Stocking and Glove" sensory loss/paresthesia. Ulcers.
    2.  **Autonomic:** Orthostatic hypotension, erectile dysfunction, urinary retention (neurogenic bladder).
*   **Pathogenesis:** High glucose $\rightarrow$ AGEs (Advanced Glycation End-products) $\rightarrow$ Microvascular damage (vasa nervorum) + Sorbitol accumulation (osmotic damage).

### 3.3 Traumatic/Compression Neuropathies
*   **Traumatic Neuroma:** Tangled mass of axons/Schwann cells after nerve transection (failed regeneration). Painful.
*   **Carpal Tunnel Syndrome:** Compression of **Median Nerve** at wrist. Numbness in thumb/index/middle fingers. Thenar atrophy.
*   **Morton's Neuroma:** Compression of interdigital nerve (usually 3rd/4th toe interspace). Related to high heels.

## 4. High Yield Renders

:::highyield
**Neurodegenerative Diseases: The "Protein & Location" Match**
| Disease | Protein Aggregate | Location of Atrophy | Key Clinical Feature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Alzheimer's** | Beta-Amyloid (Plaques), Tau (Tangles) | Temporoparietal / Global | Memory Loss |
| **Pick's (FTD)** | Tau (Pick Bodies) | Frontal / Temporal ("Knife-edge") | Personality Change > Memory |
| **Parkinson's** | Alpha-Synuclein (Lewy Bodies) | Substantia Nigra | TRAP (Tremor, Rigidity, Akinesia) |
| **Huntington's** | Huntingtin (CAG Repeats) | Caudate Nucleus | Chorea + Dementia |
| **ALS** | TDP-43 / SOD1 | UMN (Cortex) + LMN (spinal cord) | Weakness + Spasticity (No sensory loss) |
| **CJD** | Prion ($PrP^{sc}$) | Gray Matter (Spongiform) | Rapid Dementia + Myoclonus |
:::
