### Slide 47 (Score: 142.00)
```text
Summary
• There is a rational medial-lateral layout of the cranial nerve nuclei.
• The pontine trigeminal nucleus processes touch & proprioception. The spinal trigeminal 
nucleus processes pain & temperature. Most afferents are from the trigeminal nerve. Others 
from facial, glossopharyngeal, and vagus nerve.
• The facial nerve relays motor efferents from the facial motor nucleus and superior salivary 
nucleus. Relays afferents to gustatory nucleus and trigeminal nuclei. 
• The vestibulocochlear nerves uniquely supply the afferents to the  vestibular and cochlear 
nuclei. 
• The glossopharyngeal nerve is aptly named. The ambiguus nucleus controls swallowing, the 
gustatory nucleus receives tongue taste, and tongue/throat somatosensation is sent to the 
trigeminal nucleus. 
• The vagus relays autonomic input to the solitary nucleus and output from the dorsal motor 
vagus. It also relays pharyngeal and laryngeal control from the ambiguous nucleus. 
• Hypoglossal and spinal accessory nerves are motor efferents uniquely from the hypoglossal 
and spinal accessory nuclei.
```
