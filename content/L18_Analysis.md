### Slide 8 (Score: 35.00)
```text
Deformation of hair cell cilia in the direction of the kinocilium results in 
depolarization of the hair cells via opening of K+ channels on tip links.
Depolarization causes voltage gated Ca++ channels to open resulting 
in transmitter fusion, exocytosis, and transmitter release.
Binding of glutamate onto receptors on CNVIII cells causes Na+ influx 
and depolarizes these cells causing action potential generation.
Deformation of hair cell cilia in the direction opposite of the kinocilium 
results in hyperpolarization.
```

```text
Meniere Disease (PV)
● A cause of peripheral vertigo ( presents 
worse that central vertigo)
● Caused by improper endolymph resorption 
in the vestibule
● Etiology: Idiopathic, but can be due to viral 
infections, autoimmunity. Also some genetic or 
environmental factors involved. 
● Symptoms: Recurrent episodes of peripheral 
vertigo, fluctuating unilateral sensorineural 
hearing loss and unilateral tinnitus (Meniere 
triad).
● Treatment: antihistamines, benzodiazepines, 
anti-emetics.
```

```text
Labyrinthitis

the inner ear. 
• Membranous labyrinth has endolymph which has lots

• Etiology: bacterial or viral infection, and or manifestation

• Sx: severe vertigo, N/V, tinnitus, and/ or hearing loss.

residual balance or hearing issues.
• Labyrinthitis is an INFECTION of inner ear
• Meniere's is not necessarily an infection, but can be 
caused by infection. Meniere’s is also more likely to 
present with multiple recurrent episodes, unlike 
labyrinthitis.
```

```text
Vertebrobasilar Insufficiency
● Caused by inadequate blood flow through the 
posterior circulation. The medulla, cerebellum, 
pons, midbrain, thalamus and occipital cortex can 
all be affected in VBI.  2 vertebral arteries cross 
midline at C6 transverse process to become 
basilar artery. 
● It may occur as a TIA and usually causes by 
atherosclerosis of vertebral arties or basilar 
artery. It can also be caused by traveling 
embolism.

male, positive family history, obesity, diabetes 
and hyperlipidemia
● Symptoms include: Central vertigo, syncope, 
nausea, visual or auditory disturbances, 
dysphagia and hemiparesis.
```
