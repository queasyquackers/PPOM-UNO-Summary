•Geriatrics is the branch of medicine that focuses on the care of the elderly, emphasizing prevention and care of chronic illnesses.  The geriatric patient is, by convention, anyone who is ≥ 65 years old. •Aging is the inevitable, irreversible decline that occurs in organs over time even in the absence of illness, injury, or poor lifestyle choices.  This leads to changes in body composition, discrepancy in the production and utilization of energy, homeostatic dysregulation, and neurodegeneration.
