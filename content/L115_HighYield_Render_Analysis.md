 repetitive weight bearing

fracture

pain eventually leading to inability to perform an activity

with less and less activity

General Principles 
• #1 if it hurts, DO NOT DO IT
• Typically, 6 weeks of relative rest are required
• Bracing has been shown to decrease time to return to 
competition by 20 days 
• Avoid NSAIDs – may hinder bone healing
• Maintain fitness with cross training throughout rest period
• Correct identified precipitating factors
• Muscle imbalance (strength training), mechanical abnormalities 
(orthotics), dietary planning, and training modification

### Slide 19 (Score: 124.00)
```text
Focal tissue thickening and fibrosis
• Repetitive micro trauma of overuse 
•  Damage of muscle fibers
• Damage leads to the formation of adhesions between 
muscle and cross linkages in the fascia 
• These cross linkages and adhesions may be palpated 
as focal areas of tissue thickening
• Taut, thickened band arranged in the direction of stress or as 
large area of increased muscle tone
Muscle Overuse
```

### Slide 20 (Score: 133.00)
```text
Focal tissue thickening and fibrosis
• Focal areas of thickening my be responsible for
• Pain
• Predisposition to injury of tendons due to a lack of tissue 
elasticity under stretch or eccentric load 
• Decreased ability for muscle to contract and relax rapidly 
• Treatment for focal tissue thickening and fibrosis
• Soft tissue therapy (OMT)
• Strengthening 
• Stretching 
Muscle Overuse
```

### Slide 29 (Score: 167.00)
```text
Tendinopathy 
• Clinical presentation 
• Pain with use or in the morning localized to tendon 
• Typically pain free with rest
• Typical pain pattern: Pain begins with exercise, but 
decreases as patient continues to warm up, then 
returns as patient cools down 
• Physical exam: Local tenderness and thickening of the 
associated tendon
Tendon Overuse
```
