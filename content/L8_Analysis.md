### Slide 27 (Score: 145.00)
```text
Summary
•The major descending and ascending tracts have distinct trajectories through the brainstem
•The corticospinal tract is always most ventral and decussates in the lower medulla
•The DCML decussates in the upper medulla and changes position through the brainstem but 
always more medial to the spinothalamic tract.
•The spinothalamic tract decussates in the spinal cord and is always lateral to the DCML.
•The three cerebellar peduncles are present at different levels of the brainstem;  inferior – 
medulla and lower pons; middle – pons; superior – upper pons and lower midbrain.
•The cranial nerve nuclei have a rostral-caudal placement roughly aligned with the cranial 
nerves.
•Rational arrangement of cranial nerve nuclei: three motor columns are medial and three 
sensory columns are lateral.
```
