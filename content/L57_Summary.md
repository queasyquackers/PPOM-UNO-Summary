## 1. Parkinson's Disease (PD)
**Story Mode:**
Parkinson's is a disease of "Too Little Dopamine" (movement is stuck). It's a synucleinopathy, meaning the garbage protein dumping into the cells is Alpha-Synuclein. The classic patient is elderly, slow, stiff, and has a tremor.

### 1.1 Clinical Features (TRAP)
*   **T**remor: **Resting** tremor (4-6 Hz). **"Pill-rolling"**. Usually **Asymmetric** at onset.
*   **R**igidity: **"Cogwheel"** rigidity (ratchet-like resistance to passive movement).
*   **A**kinesia / Bradykinesia: **Slowness** of movement.
    *   **Masked Facies** (Hypomimia).
    *   **Micrographia** (Small handwriting).
    *   **Shuffling Gait** (Festinating gait) with reduced arm swing.
    *   **Freezing** (Feet stuck to floor, especially in doorways).
*   **P**ostural Instability: Loss of balance, frequent falls (usually a **Late** finding).

### 1.2 Non-Motor Symptoms
Often precede motor symptoms by years.
*   **Anosmia:** Loss of smell.
*   **Constipation.**
*   **REM Sleep Behavior Disorder (RBD):** Acting out dreams (punching/kicking) due to loss of REM atonia.
*   **Depression/Anxiety.**

### 1.3 Pathology & Genetics
*   **Pathology:**
    *   Loss of **Dopaminergic Neurons** in the **Substantia Nigra Pars Compacta**.
    *   **Lewy Bodies:** Intracellular eosinophilic inclusions composed of **Alpha-Synuclein**.
*   **Genetics:**
    *   **GBA (Glucocerebrosidase):** Risk factor for PD and Lewy Body Dementia. Linked to **Gaucher's Disease** (Ashkenazi Jews).
    *   **LRRK2:** Common cause of familial PD (Ashkenazi Jews, North African Berbers). Slower progression.
    *   **Parkin:** Early-onset PD (<40 yo). Autosomal Recessive.
*   **Environmental:**
    *   **Risks:** Pesticides (**Paraquat**, Rotenone), Well water, Head trauma.
    *   **Protective:** Smoking, Caffeine.

## 2. Atypical Parkinsonism (Parkinson-Plus Syndromes)
**Story Mode:**
These look like Parkinson's but "Something is Wrong." They don't respond well to Levodopa, progress faster, and have unique "Red Flags."

### 2.1 Multiple System Atrophy (MSA)
*   **Pathology:** Alpha-Synucleinopathy (Glial Cytoplasmic Inclusions).
*   **Key Features:** Parkinsonism +
    1.  **Autonomic Dysfunction (Dysautonomia):** Severe **Orthostatic Hypotension** (fainting when standing), Urinary Incontinence/Retention, Erectile Dysfunction.
    2.  **Cerebellar Signs:** Ataxia.
*   **Clinical Pearl:** Inspiratory Stridor.

### 2.2 Progressive Supranuclear Palsy (PSP)
*   **Pathology:** **Tauopathy** (Tau protein accumulation).
*   **Key Features:**
    *   **Vertical Gaze Palsy:** Cannot look **Down** (trouble reading, going down stairs).
    *   **Early Falls:** Often falling **Backwards**.
    *   **Pseudobulbar Palsy:** Dysphagia, dysarthria ("growling speech").

### 2.3 Corticobasal Degeneration (CBD)
*   **Pathology:** Tauopathy.
*   **Key Features:**
    *   **Markedly Asymmetric** symptoms.
    *   **Alien Limb Phenomenon:** One hand acts on its own (e.g., unbuttoning shirt while other buttons it).
    *   **Apraxia:** Inability to perform complex motor tasks despite motor strength.

### 2.4 Dementia with Lewy Bodies (DLB)
*   **Pathology:** Alpha-Synucleinopathy (Cortical Lewy Bodies).
*   **Key Features:**
    *   **Dementia within 1 year** of motor symptoms (or before).
    *   **Visual Hallucinations:** Early, vivid, often children or animals.
    *   **Fluctuating Cognition:** "Good days and bad days."
    *   **Severe Neuroleptic Sensitivity:** Antipsychotics cause severe worsening of parkinsonism/death.

## 3. Pharmacology of Parkinson's
**Strategy:** Increase Dopamine (Levodopa, Agonists, MAO/COMT inhibitors) or Decrease Acetylcholine (Anticholinergics).

### 3.1 Levodopa / Carbidopa
*   **Mechanism:**
    *   **Levodopa:** Precursor to Dopamine. Crosses Blood-Brain Barrier (Dopamine cannot).
    *   **Carbidopa:** **DOPA Decarboxylase Inhibitor**. Does **NOT** cross BBB. Prevents peripheral conversion of L-DOPA $\rightarrow$ Dopamine (reduces systemic side effects like nausea/arrhythmias).
*   **Use:** Gold Standard. Best for motor symptoms (bradykinesia/rigidity).
*   **Issues:**
    *   **"Wearing Off":** Effects last shorter over time.
    *   **Dyskinesias:** Chorea-like movements at peak dose.
    *   **On-Off Phenomenon:** Unpredictable fluctuations.

### 3.2 Dopamine Agonists (Pramipexole, Ropinirole, Rotigotine)
*   **Mechanism:** Directly stimulate Dopamine receptors (D2/D3).
*   **Side Effects:**
    *   **Impulse Control Disorders:** **Pathological Gambling**, Hypersexuality, Excessive Spending.
    *   **Sleep Attacks:** Sudden onset of sleep (danger while driving).
    *   Hallucinations.

### 3.3 Enzyme Inhibitors
*   **MAO-B Inhibitors (Selegiline, Rasagiline):**
    *   Inhibit breakdown of Dopamine in the brain.
    *   **Selegiline:** Metabolized to **Amphetamine** $\rightarrow$ Insomnia (don't take at night).
*   **COMT Inhibitors (Entacapone, Opicapone):**
    *   Inhibit peripheral breakdown of Levodopa (methyl-transferase).
    *   **Must be given with Levodopa**.
    *   **Side Effect:** **Orange/Dark Urine**.

### 3.4 Amantadine
*   **Mechanism:** Antiviral. **NMDA Antagonist** + Increases Dopamine release.
*   **Use:** Treating **Levodopa-induced Dyskinesias**.
*   **Side Effect:** **Livedo Reticularis** (Lacy, purplish discoloration of skin on legs).

### 3.5 Anticholinergics (Trihexyphenidyl, Benztropine)
*   **Mechanism:** Restores Dopamine/ACh balance.
*   **Use:** Predominantly for **Tremor** in younger patients ("Shake & Bake").
*   **Side Effects:** "Anti-SLUDGE" (Dry mouth, urinary retention, constipation, confusion). **Avoid in Elderly**.

## 4. High Yield Renders

:::highyield
**Parkinsonism Differential Diagnosis**
| Condition | Key Clinical Feature (The "Red Flag") | Pathology |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Parkinson's Disease** | Asymmetric, Resting Tremor, Good L-DOPA response | Synuclein (Lewy Body) |
| **MSA** | **Autonomic Failure** (Orthostasis, ED) + Cerebellar signs | Synuclein (Glial Inclusion) |
| **PSP** | **Vertical Gaze Palsy** (Can't look down) + Early **Falls** | Tau |
| **Lewy Body Dementia** | **Visual Hallucinations** + Dementia early (<1 yr) | Synuclein (Cortical Lewy Body) |
| **Corticobasal (CBD)** | **Alien Limb** + Apraxia + Severe Asymmetry | Tau |
:::
