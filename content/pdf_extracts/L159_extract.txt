Introduction to the
Pap Smear
The Basics:
Developed by George Papanicolaou
Used as a screening tool for cervical cancer
American Society for Colposcopy and Cervical Pathology (ASCCP)
Provides guidelines on screening and follow up management
Begin screening at age 21
Most cervical cancers caused by the human papilloma virus (HPV)
Credit: American Cancer Society
George Papanicolaou (1883-1962)
--- SLIDE 31 ---
Introduction to the
Pap Smear
The Anatomy/Pathology:
The cervical epithelium consists of 2 types of cells:
Columnar (glandular) – line inside of cervical canal
Squamous (stratified non keratinizing) – line outside of cervix
The area were these two meet is called the squamocolumnar junction (SCJ)
SCJ is where 90% of cervical neoplasia arise
(Based on Berek JS. Berek and Novak’s Gynecology. 15th ed.
Lippincott Williams & Wilkins; 2011.)
