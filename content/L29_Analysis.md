### Slide 27 (Score: 75.00)
```text
Neuronal Migration & Placement
• Radial glia (RG) fibers contact the pial surface forming a 
substrate onto which newborn neurons can adhere & 
migrate in an inside-outside pattern.
• Migrating neurons must recognize the molecular cues that 
instruct the direction, distance, and destination of their 
journey. Intracellular proteins like microtubule associate 
proteins contribute to shape change and movement 
capabilities of neurons to reach their appropriate layer. 
• Deficits in migration cause heterotopia and lissencephaly.
```
