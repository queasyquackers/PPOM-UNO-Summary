# Who writes what (CPR Block 3 matching game)

Every item below has exactly one owner. Write only your own; the rest of this file is
here so you know the neighbouring items your cards must be distinguishable from. Names are
working names - tidy them, but keep the same entity. `->` gives the primary lecture first,
then every other lecture that teaches it (all go in `lectures`).

You MAY add an item that your lectures teach prominently and that is not anywhere in this
file (search the whole file first). You MAY drop an item the lectures barely touch, merge two
that cannot be told apart, or split one entry into two when the lecture clearly separates
them. Report every add, drop, merge and split in your final reply.

Scope: non-OMM lectures CV116-CV163 that have a summary. Out of scope: OMM (113-115,
122-125, 133-135, 154) and 144, 145, 158 (no transcript, no summary). A lecture outside your
list may still appear in an item's `lectures` when it genuinely teaches that item.

Dropped on purpose (not conditions a vignette can test): "Chronic interstitial lung
disease" as a stand-alone item, "Glomerulonephritis and glomerular disease" (CV143's
generic block), "Nephrotic syndrome" and "Nephritic syndrome" as stand-alone items (CV147's
generic blocks - the specific diseases carry them), "Parkinson disease" (CV150, an
amantadine indication, not a block topic), "Honeymoon cystitis" as a correlate (it is plain
cystitis; the organism lives in Bugs). One-line drug adverse effects (rifampin orange
fluids, ethambutol color vision, linezolid myelosuppression) live on the drug cards, not as
correlates, unless the lecture builds a real case around them (listed below).
