# Author D notes - lecture conflicts and decisions

## Conflicts
- Latent TB regimen: CV128 Slide 9 = INH + rifampin daily x 3 months (no HIV); rifapentine weekly with HIV; rifampin x 4 months for pediatrics/pregnancy. CV131 Slide 17 = INH x 9 months OR rifampin x 4 months (single drug). Card lists the 3-month INH+RIF course with rifampin x 4 months / INH x 9 months as alternatives so both agree.
- Primary TB site: CV127 Slide 30 = granuloma in middle/lower lobes + hilar node; CV132 lecturer = lower lobe or anterior upper lobe, Slide 24 = "often upper lobe". Card uses lower/middle zone subpleural focus + hilar node (CV127 and the CV132 lecturer agree on lower lobe).
- CV138 summary correlates (MRSA, C. diff, MDR-TB, pertussis, cholera, measles, dengue, filovirus, hantavirus) carry many board-source details not on the CV138 slides/transcript (GeneXpert, LJ agar, vitamin A, ORS chemistry, ribavirin "ineffective"...). Cards built from slides/transcript only. Hantavirus: Slide 33 says ribavirin "some benefit"; the summary says ineffective - cards leave ribavirin out.

## Decisions
- "Active tuberculosis (treatment-centred case)" CV128 merged into secondary (reactivation) TB: the CV128/CV127 active pulmonary TB presentation cannot be separated from reactivation TB on any attribute.
- MAC: CV132 does not teach MAC (only CD4 thresholds), so lectures = CV128 only.
- Hansen disease: CV128 does not separate tuberculoid vs lepromatous -> one item.
- Secondary bacterial pneumonia after influenza: CV130 does not teach it (no post-influenza S. aureus content in transcript); lectures = CV141 (flu-season superinfection, S. aureus incl. MRSA and S. pneumoniae), CV132 (bacterial superinfection).
- CV129 summary adds croup dexamethasone/racemic epinephrine, ARDS prone/remdesivir; CV129 slides say croup Tx supportive (CV141: supportive, 1.5-15% hospitalized). Croup card has no management. Severe COVID ARDS management taken from CV126 Slide 48 (low TV, high PEEP, dexamethasone, anticoagulation) - CV150 gives remdesivir only to patients NOT on mechanical ventilation, so the ARDS card omits remdesivir; CV150 not listed for it.
- Dropped 'Viral pneumonia in a hospitalized patient' (CV139): the lecture gives it one line (no neutrophilia; lymphocytes rise) as the excluded answer in Case 1 - not a separable vignette; the point lives on the VAP workup card.
- Coccidioidomycosis split into primary (self-limited, shin nodules = immune reaction) and disseminated (Filipino/African American, late pregnancy, immunosuppressed; skin, bone, joints, meninges; amphotericin B / fluconazole for meningitis): CV136 separates them clinically and by treatment.
- Ebola/Marburg: CV138 Slide 32 says no approved antiviral, monoclonals REGN-EB3 and mAb114 experimental (dated: both are now FDA-approved for Ebola). Card says 'anti-glycoprotein monoclonal antibodies' without calling them approved or experimental.
- Hantavirus: CV138 Slide 33 lists ribavirin 'some benefit'; summary says ineffective. Card leaves ribavirin out (supportive ICU care only).

## Adds / drops / merges / splits vs ASSIGNMENTS.md
- Merged: "Active tuberculosis (treatment-centred case)" (CV128) into "Secondary (reactivation) tuberculosis" (lectures now CV132, CV127, CV128, CV131).
- Split: "Coccidioidomycosis (and disseminated disease)" -> "Primary pulmonary coccidioidomycosis" + "Disseminated coccidioidomycosis".
- Dropped: "Viral pneumonia in a hospitalized patient" (CV139, one line only).
- Not added: peritonsillar abscess (CV141 names it only as a strep complication), mpox (CV138 gives no clinical picture), SARS-CoV-1 2003 (cannot be separated from COVID on a card), polio, avian influenza.
- Added: "Mumps" (CV138, parotitis + orchitis + contagious window + IgM), "Acute viral laryngitis" (CV141 Slide 45: hoarseness/aphonia, red swollen larynx, voice rest + humidifier).
- Renamed: Hansen disease (leprosy); Disseminated Mycobacterium avium complex in HIV; Klebsiella pneumonia (dropped "in alcohol use disorder" - the qa leak rule would ban "alcohol" from its own card); Typical community-acquired pneumonia (outpatient); Pneumococcal pneumonia (dropped "lobar" for the same reason); Hospitalized COVID-19, not mechanically ventilated; MRSA infection; VRE infection; Ebola / Marburg virus disease.

## avoid declared
- Typical CAP (outpatient) <-> Pneumococcal pneumonia (all attributes): pneumococcus is the usual CAP organism; the two cannot be separated on every card.
- Severe COVID-19 with ARDS workup <-> F's "Acute respiratory distress syndrome"; Hantavirus workup <-> both of those (bilateral edema + normal heart).
- Aspergilloma management <-> Invasive aspergillosis (same drug list).
- VRE presentation <-> Candidemia (both ICU, line, broad antibiotics).
- Blastomycosis and Talaromycosis carry no management card (the lecture's "itraconazole / amphotericin B" is identical to histoplasmosis).
