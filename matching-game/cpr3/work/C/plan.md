# Author C plan - correlates CV116-121, CV142, CV146

Format: item | lectures | nearest differential -> separating feature

## CV116 (IIPs)
- Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis | CV116, CV119, CV120 | vs asbestosis (no job exposure, no pleural plaques), vs NSIP (older man, subpleural cysts, fibroblast foci, mixed-age scar)
- Nonspecific interstitial pneumonia | CV116 | vs IPF (woman 40s, never-smoker, CTD features, same-stage lesions, no cysts, hazy opacities)
- Desquamative interstitial pneumonia | CV116 | vs RB-ILD (macrophages fill alveolar airspaces, lamellar bodies, steroids + quit), vs PLCH (no upper-lobe cysts/nodules)
- Respiratory bronchiolitis-associated ILD | CV116 | vs DIP (macrophages in respiratory bronchiole lumens, peribronchiolar fibrosis, quitting alone); presentations avoid each other

## CV117 (sarcoid, misc ILD)
- Sarcoidosis | CV117, CV119 | vs chronic beryllium disease (no metal job; skin/eye/hilar nodes, young Black woman), vs HP (no organic-dust timing; nodes not bronchiolocentric), vs TB (no necrosis, cultures neg)
- Pulmonary Langerhans cell histiocytosis | CV117 | vs DIP/RB-ILD (upper/mid-zone nodular-web pattern; grooved S100/CD1a+ cells); mgmt avoid RB-ILD (both = quit smoking)
- Hypersensitivity pneumonitis | CV117, CV119 | vs sarcoid (episodes 4-6 h after hay/birds/hot tub; bronchiolocentric loose granulomas, precipitins), vs occupational asthma (fever, crackles, not wheeze)
- Acute eosinophilic pneumonia | CV117 | vs CEP (days, respiratory failure, BAL eos >=25% with hyaline membranes), vs ARDS (F) (no trigger; eosinophils)
- Chronic eosinophilic pneumonia | CV117 | vs secondary TB (blood eos 1,400; peripheral opacities; BAL eos >=40%), vs AEP (months, no failure)
- Pulmonary alveolar proteinosis | CV117 | vs other ILD (neutralizing anti-GM-CSF IgG; PAS+ alveolar filling with thin walls); lecture gives no clinical picture
- DROP Eosinophilic granulomatosis with polyangiitis: CV117 names it once as a cause of secondary eosinophilia (granulomas with eosinophils + angiitis, "manage underlying"); no presentation/workup taught; no other CPR3 lecture mentions it

## CV118 (pneumoconioses)
- Asbestosis | CV118, CV119 | vs IPF (shipyard/insulation job, mid-zone pleural plaques, coated rods in BAL), vs pleural plaques (symptomatic lower-zone scarring)
- Benign asbestos-related pleural plaques | CV118, CV142 | vs asbestosis (no symptoms, normal PFTs, no lung scarring), vs mesothelioma (no pain/effusion/mass)
- Chronic beryllium disease | CV118 | vs sarcoidosis (metal-machinist, older, upper lobes, mild hilar nodes, lymphocyte proliferation test to metal)
- Anthracosis | CV118 | vs simple CWP (city dweller/smoker, incidental black nodes, no macules with emphysema, normal PFTs)
- Simple coal workers' pneumoconiosis | CV118 | vs PMF (small rounded upper-zone opacities, macules <=4 mm, near-normal function), vs chronic bronchitis (miner)
- Progressive massive fibrosis | CV118 | vs simple CWP (masses >1 cm up to 10 cm, ischemic necrotic centres, mixed obstruction + high TLC/RV)
- Chronic silicosis | CV118, CV119 | vs CWP (stonemason/quarry/glass, hilar node calcium rims, whorled birefringent nodules)
- Acute silicosis (silicoproteinosis) | CV118 | vs PAP (sandblaster, weeks-months), vs chronic silicosis (tempo)
- Silicosis complicated by tuberculosis | CV118 | vs secondary TB (D) (quarry driller with known calcified nodules), vs chronic silicosis (fever, sweats, hemoptysis, AFB)
- Cor pulmonale from advanced restrictive disease | CV118, CV120 | vs isolated-low-DLCO pulmonary hypertension (right heart failure signs on top of known scarring lung)

## CV119 (obstructive/restrictive clinical)
- Emphysema-predominant COPD | CV119, CV120 | vs chronic bronchitis (thin, little sputum, DLCO 40%, TLC/RV high, normal PaCO2); mgmt avoid chronic bronchitis (same GOLD regimen)
- Chronic bronchitis | CV119, CV120 | vs emphysema (sputum >=3 mo/yr x 2 yr, cyanosis, edema, Hct 56%), vs bronchiectasis (smoker, no copious purulent sputum/hemoptysis/clubbing), vs F chronic resp acidosis in COPD (avoid)
- Bronchiectasis | CV119 | vs chronic bronchitis (cupfuls of purulent sputum daily, hemoptysis, clubbing, ciliary history; CT bronchus > artery, no tapering)
- DROP COPD exacerbation: CV119 lists exacerbation only as a complication and a GOLD-category criterion; no exacerbation management taught

## CV121 / CV120 (asthma, PFT patterns)
- Allergic asthma in a young adult | CV121, CV119, CV120 | vs EIB/occupational/obesity (eczema, hay fever, nocturnal + cat triggers; obstructive baseline reversed by albuterol >=12% and 200 mL; high IgE/eos)
- Exercise-induced bronchoconstriction | CV121 | vs allergic asthma (symptoms only 10 min into running, peak 20-30 min; normal rest spirometry; treadmill drop)
- Aspirin-exacerbated respiratory disease | CV121, CV119 | vs allergic asthma (adult, nasal polyps, attack 20 min-3 h after NSAID); mgmt LTRA + avoid NSAIDs
- Occupational asthma | CV121 | vs HP (no fever/crackles; wheeze worse on shifts, better on vacation; normal IgE; methacholine/specific-agent challenge), vs obesity asthma
- Asthma with obesity | CV121, CV120 | vs occupational (BMI 41, 67F, no work pattern; methacholine 41% drop; little eosinophilic inflammation)
- Status asthmaticus | CV121, CV119 | vs allergic asthma (failing ED therapy, drowsy, near-silent chest, rising PaCO2, inspiratory BP fall)
- Central airway tumor mimicking asthma | CV121, CV120 | vs asthma (failed every asthma drug; expiratory plateau only; mass on CT)
- Fixed upper-airway obstruction (tracheal or subglottic stenosis) | CV120 | vs vocal cord paralysis (both limbs flat, noisy breathing both ways), vs central tumor
- Vocal cord paralysis | CV120 | vs fixed (inspiratory plateau only; bulbar polio history)
- Pulmonary hypertension found on PFTs | CV120 | vs cor pulmonale (normal spirometry/volumes, DLCO alone low; echo RVSP)
- Postnasal drip | CV121 | chronic cough cause; vs GERD/ACE-i (throat drainage, nasal congestion, clearing)
- Gastroesophageal reflux disease | CV121 | chronic cough cause; vs postnasal (heartburn, sour regurgitation, worse lying down)
- ACE inhibitor adverse effect | CV121 | chronic cough cause; vs others (began lisinopril weeks before; stops after drug withdrawn)

## CV142 (lung and pleural neoplasms)
- Lung adenocarcinoma | CV142 | vs AIS (symptomatic, invasive glands, adrenal met), vs squamous (never-smoker woman, peripheral, TTF-1+, EGFR)
- Adenocarcinoma in situ | CV142 | vs adenocarcinoma (incidental <3 cm, lepidic, no invasion); presentation avoid hamartoma (both incidental nodules)
- Squamous cell lung carcinoma with hypercalcemia | CV142, CV120 | vs small cell (central cavitating mass, Ca 14.9 with low PTH, keratin pearls, PTHrP)
- Small cell lung carcinoma with ectopic ACTH | CV142 | vs SIADH twin (truncal weight gain, hypokalemia, high corticotropin/cortisol)
- Small cell lung carcinoma with SIADH | CV142 | vs ACTH twin (euvolemic Na 121, urine osm 520); workup histology overlaps -> workup avoid each other
- Superior sulcus tumor with sympathetic chain involvement | CV142 | vs SVC syndrome (apical mass, ipsilateral ptosis/miosis/anhidrosis/enophthalmos)
- Superior vena cava syndrome | CV142 | vs superior sulcus (face/arm swelling, chest-wall veins; mediastinal mass compressing SVC)
- Bronchial carcinoid tumor / carcinoid syndrome | CV142 | vs small cell (never-smoker 50, 3-cm endobronchial mass, flushing + watery stools, <2 mitoses), vs central airway tumor mimicking asthma (adult, diarrhea)
- Pulmonary hamartoma | CV142 | vs AIS (cartilage + fat + clefts, benign)
- Metastatic tumor to the lung | CV142 | vs primary lung cancers (prior colon primary, many bilateral round nodules)
- Solitary fibrous tumor of the pleura | CV142 | vs mesothelioma (no exposure, pedunculated, keratin-negative, NAB2-STAT6)
- Malignant mesothelioma | CV142, CV118 | vs pleural plaques (symptomatic, effusion, rind encasing lung), vs SFT (keratin+, calretinin+)
- DROP Large cell carcinoma: CV142 gives only histology ("waste basket", diagnosis of exclusion); presentation cannot be separated from other NSCLC
- DROP Paraneoplastic myasthenic syndrome: CV142 slide 34 "I don't emphasize this"

## CV146 (chest radiography) - every film finding described, no sign names
- Tension pneumothorax | CV146 | vs primary spontaneous (shock, shifted mediastinum, no heart right of spine)
- Primary spontaneous pneumothorax | CV146 | vs tension (stable, no shift), vs skin fold (no markings beyond line)
- Anterior pneumothorax on a supine film (ADD) | CV146 | starred film; vs other PTX (no visible line, one costophrenic angle abnormally deep and dark)
- Pneumomediastinum | CV146 | vs SC emphysema / pneumopericardium (lucency around heart running into neck, gas crossing under the heart)
- Severe subcutaneous emphysema | CV146 | vs pneumomediastinum (gas throughout chest-wall soft tissue outlining pectoral fibres)
- Right mainstem intubation | CV146 | vs misplaced NG (ET tube tip past carina; whole left lung white)
- Misplaced nasogastric tube | CV146 | vs RMS intubation (feeding catheter tip in right bronchus; new retrocardiac density after feeds)
- Pulmonary artery catheter tip too far (ADD) | CV146 | slide 30 film; tip in distal artery + wedge-shaped infarct downstream
- Pneumopericardium | CV146 | vs pneumomediastinum (post-pericardiocentesis; lucency confined to pericardium, stops at great vessels)
- Pericardial effusion | CV146 | vs cardiomegaly/edema (ratio 0.68, outline narrows abruptly at the aortic root, clear lungs)
- Cardiogenic pulmonary edema | CV146 | vs ARDS (F) (heart failure history, perihilar opacities sparing apices/bases, response to furosemide)
- Pleural effusion | CV146 | upright meniscus (presentation) vs supine veil-like haze without meniscus (workup)
- Lobar atelectasis | CV146 | vs consolidation (volume loss, raised hemidiaphragm, no air bronchograms; post-op mucus plug)
- Right middle lobe pneumonia (ADD as the consolidation item) | CV146 | vs D pneumococcal pneumonia (avoid); right heart border lost, wedge over heart on lateral, no volume loss
- Left lower lobe pneumonia (ADD) | CV146 | vs RML (left hemidiaphragm/spine border lost; lateral vertebrae whiter going down)
- Rib fractures with pulmonary contusion | CV146 | vs consolidation (trauma; airspace opacity beneath displaced breaks; >3 acute = admit)
- Hiatal hernia | CV146 | vs D lung abscess (afebrile; round gas structure with fluid level behind the heart)
- Pneumoperitoneum | CV146 | crescent of gas under right hemidiaphragm; seen anteriorly on lateral
