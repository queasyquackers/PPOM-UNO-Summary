# Agent A notes - lecture conflicts and judgment calls

## Conflicts / simplifications (phrased so card and lecture agree)
- CV71 slide 6: nitroglycerin NO release via "mitochondrial aldehyde reductase". The enzyme is
  mitochondrial aldehyde DEHYDROGENASE (ALDH2). Card says "a mitochondrial aldehyde enzyme".
- CV72 Question Review called omega-3s PPAR-gamma activators; slide 28 says PPAR-alpha. Card uses alpha.
- CV96 summary/transcript: digoxin DADs "can lead to torsades", and DADs "after phase 3"; CV85 says
  "after phase 4". Torsades is an EAD (long-QT) rhythm. Card says only "delayed-afterdepolarization
  arrhythmias"; magnesium card keeps both indications (torsades, digitalis arrhythmias) as the slides do.
- CV96 transcript: milrinone "blocks the breakdown of protein kinase A" (misspeak); slide/summary say cAMP. Card uses cAMP.
- CV96: SGLT2 inhibitors "not for CrCl < 30" with lecturer noting it may now be 20. Card: "below about 20-30 mL/min".
- CV96: nesiritide "discontinued". It was largely abandoned after renal-harm and no-benefit data. Card: "abandoned".
- CV63 lists fibrates as "inhibit hepatic release of VLDL" (and clofibrate); CV72 gives PPAR-alpha/LPL. Card uses CV72.
- CV96 attributes hydralazine's action to raised cGMP (mechanism is not fully established). Card keeps "raises smooth-muscle cGMP".

## Facts added beyond the lecture (standard board facts, none contradict it)
- Prasugrel (lecture only lists it): not 2C19-dependent, more bleeding, avoid after stroke/TIA and at age 75+.
- Ticagrelor: dyspnea, ventricular pauses, uric acid rise, high-dose aspirin blunts benefit. Cangrelor: offset ~1 h.
- Ticlopidine: TTP. Abciximab group: thrombocytopenia. Cilostazol: HF contraindication, headache/diarrhea.
- Dipyridamole: adenosine reuptake block, reversed by aminophylline.
- Dronedarone: worse outcomes in advanced/decompensated HF and permanent AF; liver injury.
- Esmolol: RBC esterases, t1/2 ~9 min. Nebivolol AEs. Labetalol/carvedilol AEs.
- Magnesium toxicity signs. Mexiletine tremor/dizziness. Phenytoin CYP induction, teratogenicity.
- Aliskiren diarrhea and no dual RAAS block in diabetes; ARNI 36-hour ACEi washout.
- Dopamine dose ranges/extravasation; dobutamine tolerance; milrinone renal dosing.
- Vericiguat embryo-fetal toxicity; conivaptan osmotic demyelination/CYP3A4; semaglutide GI, pancreatitis, C-cell warning.
- SGLT2 genital infections and euglycemic ketoacidosis.
- Inclisiran AEs, evinacumab nasopharyngitis/fetal toxicity, omega-3 fishy taste/AF, bempedoic liver-only activation.

## Group policy
A group is assigned where that group's lecture teaches the drug as therapy or stresses it as a
contraindication there (verapamil/diltiazem "not for CHF" -> HF; pindolol/acebutolol "Worsen!!!" -> HF).
Deck-review questions (amiodarone in CV96, acebutolol in CV72) and one-line recap lists (esmolol,
nebivolol, adenosine, nonselective blockers on CV96 slides 25-26) do not add a group.
