# E notes (lecture conflicts / simplifications)

- CV86 hypomagnesemia: the lecturer explains refractory hypokalemia as low Mg inhibiting Na+/K+-ATPase so K+ stays outside cells and is excreted. The standard explanation is loss of Mg2+ block of ROMK channels in the distal nephron (more K+ secretion). Cards state only the clinical behaviour (K+ will not rise until Mg replaced), so both agree.
- CV87 Gitelman/Bartter: lecture files both under "inherited renal magnesium wasting". Bartter more typically has normal or only mildly low Mg2+; card gives low-normal Mg2+ and separates them on urine calcium (lecture's stated discriminator) and age.
- CV87 humoral hypercalcemia: ASSIGNMENTS lists CV96, but CV96 only mentions loop diuretics as a treatment for hypercalcemia of malignancy; lectures kept as CV87 only.
- CV92 TAPVR: lecture lists "prostaglandin to keep the PDA open" with surgery. In obstructed TAPVR prostaglandin is of limited/controversial value and surgery is the emergency; card says surgery first and that the shunts must stay open until repair, so it does not contradict the lecture.
