# Who writes what (CPR Block 2 matching game)

Every item below has exactly one owner. Write only your own; the rest of this file is
here so you know the neighbouring items your cards must be distinguishable from. Names are
working names - tidy them, but keep the same entity. `->` gives the primary lecture first,
then every other lecture that teaches it (all go in `lectures`).

You MAY add an item that your lectures teach prominently and that is not anywhere in this
file (search the whole file first). You MAY drop an item the lectures barely touch, or
split one entry into two when the lecture clearly separates them. Report every add, drop
and split in your final reply. OMM lectures (55-57, 68-69, 79-80, 90, 100-103) are out of
scope.

Dropped on purpose (not conditions a vignette can test): "Leading causes of CV death",
"Diet-related chronic disease", "Overweight and obesity", "Colon cancer (low fiber)",
"Atherosclerosis" as a stand-alone item, "Fat malabsorption", "Hypercholesterolemia and
cholesterol-lowering strategies", "Liver disease and bile acids", "Exercise-associated K+
shifts", "Superimposed acute illness", "CHF as a contraindication", "Stable angina - which
beta blocker worsens it", "Hypertension (dietary management)", "Omega-3 therapy". Drug
adverse effects that are just one AE (gingival hyperplasia, ticlopidine neutropenia,
niacin/bempedoic gout, fibrate gallstones, bronchospasm from nonselective beta blockers)
live on the drug cards, not as correlates.

## A - Drugs (all of them)

Sources: CV71 (Angina), CV72 (Lipid-lowering), CV85 (Antiarrhythmics), CV96 (Heart
failure); CV95 for how GDMT is used, CV74/CV75 only to confirm clinical use. Cover every
drug or tight class those four lectures teach (expect roughly 70-80 items). Groups:
- Antiarrhythmics: classes IA, IB, IC, II, III, IV and the "other" agents CV85 teaches
  (adenosine, digoxin, magnesium, etc.)
- Angina: nitrates, beta blockers, calcium channel blockers, ranolazine, ivabradine if
  taught there, and the antiplatelet / antithrombotic drugs CV71 teaches
- Heart failure: everything CV96 teaches (RAAS drugs, ARNI, beta blockers used in HF,
  MRAs, SGLT2 inhibitors, diuretics, hydralazine/nitrate, digoxin, inotropes, vasodilators,
  newer agents)
- Lipid-lowering: everything CV72 teaches
A drug taught in more than one of those lectures is ONE item carrying every group.
Drugs taught only outside these lectures (heparin for PE, thrombolytics in CV73) are out.
